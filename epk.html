About the band

Turn it up.

Heartland Mile is a four-piece Chicagoland roots rock and Americana cover band. Drawing from Tom Petty, Bob Seger, Creedence Clearwater Revival, Van Morrison, the Allman Brothers and more, the band brings a loose, lively feel to familiar songs.

The members are experienced Chicago-area musicians whose stages have included Hey Nonny, Bub City, Joe’s Live and The Acorn Theater.
