About the band

Four Chicago musicians. One live-band sound.

Heartland Mile plays roots rock, Americana and familiar crowd favorites with ringing guitars, big choruses and a solid groove.

Collectively, members of Heartland Mile have played respected Chicagoland stages including Hey Nonny, Bub City, Joe’s Live and The Acorn Theater.
