About the band

Built for the room, not the algorithm.

Heartland Mile is a four-piece band of experienced Chicago musicians playing roots rock, Americana, and crowd-favorite songs with a warm, road-trip feel and a lived-in bar-band soul.

These are the tunes you roll the windows down for — the songs that sound right on a patio at sunset, in a packed neighborhood bar, or on the drive home with the night still warm. Heartland Mile keeps it loose, musical, and real: no overcooked gimmicks, just a band that knows how to read a room and make it feel good.

Between the four members, the band has played respected Chicagoland stages and listening rooms including Hey Nonny, Bub City, Joe’s Live, and The Acorn Theater.
